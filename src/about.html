The Art of Custom Apparel

ThirtyOne Lab is a custom apparel shop in Kajang, Selangor. We combine high-performance sports fabrics with clean, minimalist designs to create custom teamwear, uniforms, and streetwear.

We design every layout, stitch pattern, and print option with care. Good apparel does more than keep you covered. It gives you the confidence of wearing gear that represents your team.
